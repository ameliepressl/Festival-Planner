# Needs attention

## Why

Surface overdue and upcoming work from Project tasks.

## What

List unfinished subtasks due through seven calendar days from today in Lisbon, ordered by due date. Show the title, work area, and deadline. Open and focus the corresponding Task Board card on click.

## Out of scope

Dependencies, assignments, and accounts.

## Done when

The overview reflects saved plan dates and board statuses, and each listed task opens its card.

## Deadline filters and grouping

Group the overview by overdue, today, and the next seven days. Add a board filter for unfinished subtasks due today through seven calendar days ahead. Show relative deadlines alongside dates.
