# One example artist for new plans

Why: give people a clear starting point without a pre-filled list of suggested artists.

What: new plans start with one editable Example Artist, with a €0 fee and 60-minute set. Existing saved catalogues and bookings remain available, including older saved bookings from before custom catalogues existed.

Out of scope: resetting browser data, committing, or pushing.

Done when: new-plan defaults, saved-plan preservation, booking and add/edit checks pass, and the example is visible in a fresh preview.
