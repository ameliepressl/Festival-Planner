# Overview

## Why

Give organisers one starting screen for event details and progress.

## What

- Open Overview by default and list it first in the sidebar.
- Show the festival name, venue or city, dates and default opening hours in a centered grid.
- Prompt for these details on first setup and reopen the dialog from the festival options menu.
- Show booked performances, remaining artist budget and final checklist progress.
- Link to the lineup planner and final checklist.
- Save a local team roster with Admin, Editor and Viewer roles and their planned permissions.

## Out of scope

Shared plans, accounts, invitations and permission enforcement.

## Done when

The setup dialog opens before details are saved and the options menu can reopen it afterward. Team entries and festival details persist after reload, calendar dates and hours reflect the settings, and changes that conflict with existing appointments explain the conflict and leave the saved plan intact.
